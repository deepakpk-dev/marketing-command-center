# Visual system

## Scene and theme

A marketer compares campaigns during a morning review on a laptop beside a sunlit window. A light workspace keeps long sessions readable.

## Color strategy

Restrained warm-neutral surfaces with a leaf-green accent for selection and primary actions. Data uses green for revenue and muted lavender for spend. Semantic amber and red signal warnings and deterioration.

## Tokens

Background oklch(0.975 0.005 100); surface oklch(0.995 0.002 100); sidebar oklch(0.956 0.007 110); text oklch(0.25 0.025 155); muted oklch(0.52 0.013 150); accent oklch(0.48 0.12 153); border oklch(0.90 0.009 120).

## Typography

System sans family. Labels 12px, body 14px, section headers 17px, page heading 30px. Tabular figures on metrics. Sentence case throughout.

## Layout and components

228px sidebar, compact toolbar, 32px workspace padding, a flat KPI strip, chart and analyst split, campaign table, inline approval details. Small full borders, 12px panel radius, minimal elevation. Buttons share 8px radii and explicit hover, focus, busy, and disabled states.

## Motion

Only state changes, 180ms ease out. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
