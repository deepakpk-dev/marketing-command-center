# Signal

## Register

product

## Users

Performance marketers and growth leads reviewing campaign exports, plus recruiters, startup founders and curious visitors evaluating the product without advertising credentials. They typically use a laptop in a bright office. Marketers need to spot waste and understand changes; evaluators need to experience the complete evidence-to-decision workflow immediately.

## Product Purpose

Connect marketing data to decisions. A portfolio application demonstrates ingesting advertising and site data, measuring outcomes, detecting changes, explaining evidence, and recording human decisions.

## Brand Personality

Precise, composed, practical. Voice is short and direct. Use Signal as a compact product name, with the full project name in repository documentation.

## Anti-references

Decorative AI chat bubbles, neon command consoles, glass panels, indiscriminate card grids, and unsupported certainty about attribution or causation.

## Design Principles

Keep the result and evidence together. Make the next action obvious. Offer sample exploration and browser-local report upload without mandatory accounts or tours. Distinguish sample, uploaded and private workspace data, and clearly label simulations. Give tables enough room. Guidance should be contextual, bite-sized and actionable; explain measurement caveats at the point of use.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA: visible focus, semantic headings and tables, keyboard access, descriptive labels, color-independent status, reduced motion support, responsive layouts.
