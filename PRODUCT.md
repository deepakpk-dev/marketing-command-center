# Signal

## Register

product

## Users

Performance marketers and growth leads reviewing campaigns on a laptop in a bright office. Their daily job is to spot waste, understand changes, and approve evidence-backed adjustments.

## Product Purpose

Connect marketing data to decisions. A portfolio application demonstrates ingesting advertising and site data, measuring outcomes, detecting changes, explaining evidence, and recording human decisions.

## Brand Personality

Precise, composed, practical. Voice is short and direct. Use Signal as a compact product name, with the full project name in repository documentation.

## Anti-references

Decorative AI chat bubbles, neon command consoles, glass panels, indiscriminate card grids, and unsupported certainty about attribution or causation.

## Design Principles

Keep the result and evidence together. Make the next action obvious. Distinguish modeled sample data from live data. Give tables enough room. Explain measurement caveats at the point of use.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA: visible focus, semantic headings and tables, keyboard access, descriptive labels, color-independent status, reduced motion support, responsive layouts.
